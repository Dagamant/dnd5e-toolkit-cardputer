# Sentient Item's Special Purpose (SRD 5.2.1)
# die: d10
1	Aligned. The item seeks to defeat or destroy those of a diametrically opposed alignment. Such an item is never Neutral.
1	Bane. The item seeks to thwart or destroy creatures of a particular type, such as Constructs, Fiends, or Undead.
1	Creator Seeker. The item seeks its creator and wants to understand why it was created.
1	Destiny Seeker. The item believes it and its bearer have key roles to play in future events.
1	Destroyer. The item craves destruction and goads its user to fight arbitrarily.
1	Glory Seeker. The item seeks renown as the greatest magic item in the world by winning fame or notoriety for its user.
1	Lore Seeker. The item craves knowledge or is determined to solve a mystery, learn a secret, or unravel a cryptic prophecy.
1	Protector. The item seeks to defend a particular kind of creature, such as elves or werewolves.
1	Soulmate Seeker. The item seeks another sentient magic item, perhaps one that is similar to itself.
1	Templar. The item seeks to defend the servants and interests of a particular deity.
